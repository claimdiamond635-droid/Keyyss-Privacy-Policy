About KEYYSS

KEYYSS is the name used for a brand and related projects, including a customer support channel. This page provides information for users who want to understand how information may be handled during support interactions.

This is a static information page. It does not provide an account registration form and does not ask visitors to enter a password to read the policy.

Different bots, websites, integrations, and third-party services may operate separately. Mentioning KEYYSS does not mean every service shares the same systems, operators, or data practices.

Transparency note: the operator's legal name and business contact details should be added here if applicable and verified.
01

Scope of this policy

This policy covers information handling in KEYYSS support interactions, such as asking questions, reporting technical issues, requesting assistance, or providing feedback.

It does not replace the privacy policies of Telegram, GitHub Pages, hosting providers, payment providers, or other external platforms. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

When may it apply?

  • When you contact a KEYYSS support account.
  • When you provide details to explain a problem.
  • When support staff follow up on a request or keep relevant support records.

If a particular product has additional data practices, a separate notice should be provided where appropriate.

02

Information we may handle

The information involved depends on the platform, the features in use, and what you choose to submit. Please provide only the minimum information needed to resolve your request.

Messages and reports

Questions, complaints, replies, timestamps, and context included in a conversation.

Platform identifiers

Display name, username, platform account ID, or contact details made available during an interaction.

Attachments

Screenshots, images, documents, or other files you voluntarily submit.

Technical information

Access or error logs that infrastructure providers may record for operation and security.

Support records

Request status, problem summaries, and follow-up notes needed to handle a case.

Unexpected sensitive data

Sensitive information may appear in a message or attachment if you submit it unintentionally.

Never send these through support chat

  • Passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, or security answers
